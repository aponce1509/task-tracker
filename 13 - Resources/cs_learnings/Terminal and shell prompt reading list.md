# [[Terminal and shell prompt reading list]]

> [!abstract] Reading list
> - Books, articles and standards on terminals, ttys, pseudoterminals, shells and prompts.
> - Chapter titles are cited, not numbers.

## Books

- *The Linux Programming Interface*, Michael Kerrisk. Chapters on terminals and pseudoterminals. Covers [[tty]], [[pseudoterminal]]. #todo verify chapters
- *Advanced Programming in the UNIX Environment*, W. Richard Stevens and Stephen Rago. Chapters on terminal I/O and pseudo terminals. Covers [[tty]], [[pseudoterminal]]. #todo verify chapters
- *The UNIX Programming Environment*, Brian Kernighan and Rob Pike. The Unix model and the [[shell]].
- *The Art of UNIX Programming*, Eric S. Raymond. Historical context for [[teletype and video terminal]].
- *From Bash to Z Shell*, Oliver Kiddle, Jerry Peek and Peter Stephenson. The zsh [[shell]] and the [[shell prompt]].

## Articles and standards

- "The TTY demystified", Linus Åkesson. Explains [[tty]] and the line discipline.
- ECMA-48, Control Functions for Coded Character Sets. The standard behind [[ANSI escape sequence]].
- XTerm Control Sequences (Invisible Island). The sequences a [[terminal emulator]] accepts.
