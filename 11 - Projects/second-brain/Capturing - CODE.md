# [[Capturing - CODE]]


> [!note] Definition
> Capture is the process of externalising information and thoughts to free up cognitive space. It involves acting as a filter for both external inputs and internal sparks, ensuring that nothing valuable is lost to forgetfulness.

## Sources of Capture

1. **External Inputs (The World Around You)**:
    - Art and visual inspiration.
    - Music and auditory clips.
    - Movies and video content.
    - Books, articles, and newsletters.
    - Personal comments or critiques on any of the above.

2. **Internal Inputs (The Mind's Creation)**:
    - Random ideas or "lightbulb" moments.
    - Deep personal reflections.
    - Potential product ideas or business concepts.
    - Sudden solutions to complex problems previously encountered.

## Principles of Effective Capture

1. **The "Touch it Once" Rule**: Try to capture directly into your trusted system rather than leaving it in a temporary spot.
2. **Criteria for Capturing**: Only save things that are surprising, useful, inspiring, or personal.
3. **Low Friction**: The tool used for capturing must be fast enough to keep up with the speed of thought.
