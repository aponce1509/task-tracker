# [[Organise - CODE]]

## Definition

Organising is the act of putting captured information where it will be most useful for future action. Unlike traditional filing, it is not about where you *found* the information, but where you will *use* it.

## The PARA Method

The most common framework for organising for actionability [[PARA Methodology]]:

1. **Projects**: Active efforts with a specific deadline (e.g., "Write Q1 Report").
2. **Areas**: Ongoing responsibilities that require a standard over time (e.g., "Health," "Finances").
3. **Resources**: Topics of ongoing interest or research (e.g., "Typography," "Gardening").
4. **Archives**: Completed projects or inactive items from the other three categories.

## Guiding Principles

1. **Organise for Action**: Ask yourself, "In which project will this be most useful?" rather than "What category does this belong to?"
2. **Avoid Over-Categorisation**: Do not create complex folder structures for information you don't need yet; keep it flat and searchable.
3. **The "Wait" Strategy**: Don't feel pressured to organise everything immediately; some notes can stay in an "In-box" until their purpose becomes clear.

## Relationship with Divergence

- Organising acts as the bridge between **Divergence** (Capture) and **Convergence** (Distill).
- It provides the workspace where "chaotic" captured thoughts begin to find their structure.
