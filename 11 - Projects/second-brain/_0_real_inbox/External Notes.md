# [[External Notes]]

## Definition

> [!note] 
> 
> - Notes originated from information outside of one's own primary thoughts or internal dialogue.
> - Characterized by the use of the tag `#_external`

## Content Types

1. **Quotes**: Verbatim transcriptions of impactful phrases or sentences.
2. **Principal Ideas**: Summaries of the core arguments or concepts found in the source material.
3. **Annotations**: Personal commentary or brief observations derived directly from the external context.

## Best Practices

1. Include a reference or a brief comment regarding the source of the information.
2. Keep the content objective to distinguish it from personal "permanent" notes.
3. Apply the `#external` tag consistently at the top or bottom of the file.
