Replaces [[WORKFLOWS - Distillation of a Note]]. Driven by `/guide-distillation`.

Truths
- Goal: the summary alone tells me what the note says
- Input: one note with `maturity: capture`
- Steps
	- Contextualise: links, backlinks, `paras`, type and subtypes
	- Diagnose by the kind of the note
		- Summary form: summary, abstract, definition or take-aways (active decision)
		- Format of the kind: LaTeX for concepts, bullets for indexes, prose for MoCs
		- Noise to prune, typos, broken or missing links
		- Indexes or MoCs that should link it
	- Show the new top callouts and the list of changes, approve
	- Apply, keep my wording where it is clear
	- `maturity: capture` → `distilled`; the note keeps its folder
	- Offer the index or MoC link when missing
- Style comes from [[How I Like My Notes]]: callouts on top, ASD-STE100, no comments to me
- Changes against the legacy version: no "bold the key phrases" rule by default, the form depends on the kind

Open questions
- Is bold still wanted, or only the summary callout
- When does a note go from `distilled` to `evergreen`, and who decides
- Does a distillation pass split a note that holds two ideas (concepts), or only propose it
