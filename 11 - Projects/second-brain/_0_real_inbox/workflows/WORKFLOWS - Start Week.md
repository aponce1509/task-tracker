# [[WORKFLOWS - Start Week]]

> [!summary]
> Design the weekly sandbox. This note holds all tasks eligible for execution over the next 7 days.

**⏱️ Timecap: 10 Minutes**

Every Monday morning (or Sunday evening):
1. **Review the Past Week:** Open the previous `[[Week Note]]`. Drag any rollover tasks that still matter into the new week. Delete or archive the ones that don't.
2. **Consult Active Projects:** Look at your active P/A/R notes. Pull relevant milestones or next steps into this week's master task list.
3. **Set the Week's Focus:** Define the "North Star" goal for the week. 
4. **Brain Dump New Work:** Add any new tasks, ideas, or commitments that need to happen this week into your weekly backlog section.
