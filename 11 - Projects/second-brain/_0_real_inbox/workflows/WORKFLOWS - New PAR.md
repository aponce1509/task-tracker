# [[WORKFLOWS - New PAR]]

> [!summary] Summary
> Projects, Areas, and Resources are the structural pillars of the
> vault. Setting them up clearly prevents future clutter.

When creating a new Project, Area, or Resource:

1. **Define the Anchor:** State the clear objective (Project), the tracking
   metric/boundaries (Area), or the primary information sources/syllabus
   (Resource).
2. **The 2-Minute Brain Dump:** Write a bulleted list of your _current_
   knowledge, questions, ideas, and immediate next steps. Link to any relevant
   existing notes or resources. Do not try to make a perfect outline yet.
3. **Connect the Dots:** Link it to its parent Area or relevant
   Projects/Resources in the metadata.
4. **Trigger First Action:** For Projects, immediately schedule or block out the
   very next actionable step in your calendar or task manager.
