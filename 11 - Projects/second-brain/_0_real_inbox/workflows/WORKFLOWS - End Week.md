# [[WORKFLOWS - End Week]]

> [!summary]
> Assess your workload accuracy, clear out lingering items, and close the weekly loop.

**⏱️ Timecap: 10 Minutes**

Every Friday afternoon (or Sunday evening):
1. **Audit the Workload:** Look at your master weekly task list. Did you overcommit? Did you undercommit? 
2. **Clear the Workspace:** Check off finished items. If a task was pushed day after day and never touched, delete it or send it back to a deep backlog (Resource/Area note).
3. **Weekly Rate:** Score your productivity, focus, and alignment in the frontmatter `rate:` field.
4. **Log the Highlights:** Write down 2–3 major milestones or lessons learned this week to fuel your long-term review engine.
