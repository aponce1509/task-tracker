# [[WORKFLOWS - Distillation of a Note]]

> [!summary]
> Distillation increases the "signal-to-noise" ratio of a note so your future self can understand it in 3 seconds.

When distilling a captured note (`#_capture`):
1. **Prune the Noise:** Delete generic fluff, duplicate thoughts, or irrelevant quotes. Keep only the core insights.
2. **Increase Scannability:** 
   - **Bold** the most critical phrases.
   - Use headings (`##`) and bullet points to break up blocks of text.
3. **Create the Callout:** Write a brief 1-2 sentence executive summary inside a `> [!summary]` callout block at the top of the note.
4. **Upgrade the Status:** Change the tag from `#_capture` to `#_distill`.
