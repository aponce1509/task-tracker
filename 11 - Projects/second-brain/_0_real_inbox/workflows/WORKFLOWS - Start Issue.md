# [[WORKFLOWS - Start Issue]]

> [!summary]
> Slow down to speed up. Do not touch the problem until it is clearly defined and broken down into frictionless micro-steps.

When opening a new active Issue note:
1. **Define the "Why":** Write a clear 2-3 sentence description of the current problem, error, or blocker. What is the current behavior vs. the expected behavior?
2. **Deconstruct to Sandbox Steps:** Break down the resolution into a checklist of small, progressive steps. *Rule of thumb: Each step should take less than 30 minutes to execute.*
3. **Link Dependencies:** Connect the issue note to its parent Project or relevant technical Resource documentation.
4. **Initialize the Timestamp:** Add your first timestamped entry (`### 2026-05-16 17:15 - Initialization`) in the issue log/journal section to begin tracking your thoughts.
