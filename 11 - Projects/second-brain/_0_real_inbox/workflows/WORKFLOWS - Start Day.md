# [[WORKFLOWS - Start Day]]

> [!summary]
> A 5-minute setup to align your daily focus with the weekly plan and leverage yesterday's momentum.

**Timecap: 5 Minutes**

Every morning when opening your Daily Note:
1. **Cross the Hemingway Bridge:** Look at the "Next Step" or open loop left at the bottom of yesterday's note. Execute or continuous that specific thought immediately to build fast momentum.
2. **Consult the Week Note:** Open the current `[[Week Note]]` to see the macro workload and priorities.
3. **Write Tasks by Hand:** Manually type out 1 major objective and a maximum of 3–5 supporting tasks for today. *If it's not written here, it doesn't exist for today.*
4. **Calendar Check:** Quickly glance at your calendar for meetings or hard time blocks to ensure your task list fits your available free time.
