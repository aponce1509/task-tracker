# [[WORKFLOWS - End PAR]]

> [!summary] Summary
> Closing a note ensures the vault remains a clean representation of your
> current life, while retaining valuable lessons.

When a Project, Area, or Resource is finished, paused, or abandoned:

1. **Triage Immediately:**
	- _Delete_ if it was barely developed or holds no future value.
	- _Archive_ if it contains reference material or historical data.
2. **Post-Mortem**: [[Postmortem Content]]
	- Look for incomplete Issues, Tech Debts, Bugs and todos. Document this list when retaking the project.
	- Write a final MoC with the core concepts and elements of your solution. It's important to know the intermediate products. This note will be used mostly just for looking reusable content.
	- Rate your performance/satisfaction in the `rate:` frontmatter (1-3 stars).
	- Write exactly **one** sentence on what went well, and **one** sentence on what could be improved.
3. **Freeze the Note:** Change its `active` property to false and move it to
   your `Archive/` folder.
