# [[External Notes]]

> [!abstract] Notes about what others think
> - An external note holds information from outside my own thoughts: a book, an article, a video, a chat.
> - Its identity is its type (External Knowledge cluster or Annotations), never a tag.
> - It always carries its source and stays objective; my own view goes in a separate, marked part.

> [!warning] Legacy tag
> - The old `#_external` / `#external` tag is retired. Do not add it; replace it on sight by the type.

## Definition

A note that comes from information outside my own primary thoughts or internal dialogue.

## Metadata

- `type`: `[[Medias]]` (book, film, podcast, show) or `[[Resources]]` (article, paper, video, chats…) for the source itself; `[[Annotations]]` (extract, question, evidence, conclusion) for what I take from it. See [[Information Architecture & Taxonomy Model]].
- `paras`: the project, area or resource it serves.
- Place: `06 - Inputs/` for references, next to its PAR otherwise ([[Organise - CODE]]).

## Content

1. **Quotes**: verbatim transcriptions of impactful phrases or sentences.
2. **Main ideas**: summaries of the core arguments or concepts of the source.
3. **Annotations**: personal commentary or short observations from the source context.

## Rules

1. Link the source: the reference note (for example [[Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte]]) or its URL.
2. Keep the content objective, to separate it from my own permanent notes.
3. Capture only what passes the filter: [[Capture Criteria How to Avoid Keeping Too Much (or Too Little)]].
