# [[Convergence and Divergence]]

> [!abstract] Two modes of thinking, one rhythm
> - Divergence opens: gather, explore, accept chaos. Quantity over quality.
> - Convergence closes: filter, synthesise, ship one outcome. Focus over breadth.
> - The CODE loop moves from divergence (capture) to convergence (distill, express).

![[Pasted image 20260215190445.png]]

## Definition

1. **Divergence**: the expanding phase. I gather information, explore perspectives and make broad connections. Quantity over quality.
2. **Convergence**: the narrowing phase. I filter, synthesise and organise the findings into one outcome. Focus, utility, closed loops.

| Phase | Goal | Mindset |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Divergence** | Exploration | Curious, non-judgmental, broad |
| **Convergence** | Execution | Critical, selective, focused |

## In the CODE loop

[[The CODE system]] moves between the two states:

1. **Capture (divergence)**: collect snippets, ideas and bookmarks without judgment → [[Capturing - CODE]].
2. **Organise (transition)**: put notes in broad contexts (PARA) to see how they relate to the projects → [[Organise - CODE]].
3. **Distill (convergence)**: find the signal in the noise with [[Progressive Summarisation]] → [[Distill - CODE]].
4. **Express (convergence)**: funnel the research into one product → [[Express - CODE]], [[Intermediate and Final Products]].

Convergence needs tools to stay on: [[Keeping the Focus]].
