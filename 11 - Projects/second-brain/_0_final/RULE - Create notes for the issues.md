# [[RULE - Create notes for the issues]]

> [!abstract] Spend time on the issue creation
> - Every piece of work gets its own issue note.
> - A clean, prioritised list of issues tells me what to do and keeps me on it.
> - Writing the issue is the divergence; doing it is the convergence.

> [!important]
> - Spend some time on the issue creation. It applies [[Convergence and Divergence]]: [[Cuando aprendas define fase de divergencia y convergencia]].

A list of issues, clean and with priorities, helps me to:

- Decide what I must do to complete the project.
- Keep track of the things I can do for the project.
- Keep the focus on the task to complete — [[Keeping the Focus]].
- Force myself to separate the divergence and the convergence workflows.

How: [[The Issue System]] · `/create-issue`. Why issues and not a to-do list: [[This is really a Task]].
