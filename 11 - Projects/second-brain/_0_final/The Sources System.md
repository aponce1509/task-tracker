# [[The Sources System]]

> [!abstract] From the feeds to the notes: inputs, classification, storage, reading
> - Inputs: the feeds I consume and the sources that shaped the system.
> - Classification: important for learning, leisure or casual reading.
> - Storage: Zotero for learning references, Reader as the reading inbox, the media database for books and films.
> - Reading: one colour code for the highlights; what passes the filter becomes an external note.

## Inputs

Material enters from feeds: YouTube, Twitter, Instagram, Pinterest and the news ([[Input sources]]). The list of feeds is still open. From the phone, one share Shortcut and the Apple Notes folder `Vault` feed the capture importer; it writes capture notes to `06 - Inputs/Captures/` ([[HOWTO - Capture from the phone]]). The system itself grew from a few sources: Tiago Forte, Steph Ango and some vault tours ([[Inspiration Sources for Note Management]]).

## Classification

Each reference is important for learning, leisure or casual reading ([[Managing online References]]). Leisure and casual reading fill the moments when I do not want to learn. A learning reference serves a task or a learning project ([[The Learning System]]).

## Storage

Learning references go to Zotero, my own internet; the notes are my notebook ([[Zotero - The ultimate reference manager]]). Reader is only the inbox and the app to consume the internet. Books, films, podcasts and shows go to the media database: one `[[Medias]]` note each, with its `consumption_status` and my opinion ([[References Database]]).

## Reading

I highlight with one colour code: red and blue first, then yellow, then green for the cards to learn ([[Notes Colours Rules]]). What passes the filter of [[Capture Criteria How to Avoid Keeping Too Much (or Too Little)]] becomes an external note ([[External Notes]]). From there it enters [[The CODE system]].
