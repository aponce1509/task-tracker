# [[WORKFLOWS - Start Day]]

> [!summary]
> A five-minute setup that turns yesterday's bridge and the active projects into a short plan in the daily note. What is not written in the daily note does not exist today.

**Trigger:** the first time I open the vault in the day · **Timecap:** 5 minutes · **Driven by:** the `task-planner` skill

## Steps

- Open the daily note and the weekly note.
- Cross the bridge: read the `Next:` line at the end of yesterday's Daily Log ([[The Hemingway Bridge]]). Start with it when it still holds.
- Ask the `task-planner` for the ordered tree of candidate tasks. It reads the active PARs, their open issues, dependencies, priorities and due dates.
- Write the plan under the Daily Log: one main objective and three to five tasks, linked to their issues.
	- Check the calendar first. The plan fits the free time, not the wish list.
- Look at the inbox count, nothing more. A free slot later in the day can take one batch of [[WORKFLOWS - Out of the Inbox]].
- Set the workplace: close the tabs that are not the first task ([[Alejandro's Mise-en-Place]]).

## Tidies

- Nothing directly. It decides which notes the day's use will tidy.
