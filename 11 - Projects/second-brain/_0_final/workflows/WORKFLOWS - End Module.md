# [[WORKFLOWS - End Module]]

> [!summary]
> A module ends when I reviewed all its content, applied it, and its concepts are in the vault and recalled. Then the syllabus moves one step and I know what comes next.

**Trigger:** I read all the parts of the `[/]` module · **Timecap:** 30 minutes · **Driven by:** the `note-syllabus` skill, `/new-concept`, `/recall`

## Steps

- Review: every part of the `Read:` line is read. I do not stop a module until I review all its content.
- Homework: I apply the module once (a test, an exercise, a small piece of a project). Learn fast, build fast ([[How to be an effective self-learner]]).
- Concepts: `/new-concept` on what the sessions did not capture yet ([[WORKFLOWS - Learning Session]]).
- Recall: `/recall` on the concepts of the module.
- Close the module: `Concepts:` line under it, mark it `[x]`.
- `winstreak`: +1 when the module fit in its week (max 5), 0 when it did not.
- Parked: what I found out of the scope goes to `## Parked`.
- Next: the next module starts with [[WORKFLOWS - Start Module]]. After the last one, `learning_status: learned` and `status: done`.

## Tidies

- Unrecalled concepts of the module.
- The syllabus: the plan follows the real learning, out-of-scope ideas are parked, not lost.
