# [[WORKFLOWS - Distillation of a Note]]

> [!summary]
> Distillation raises the signal of one note until its summary alone tells me what it says. The form of the summary and the format depend on the kind of the note. The note keeps its place and my voice; only its form and its `maturity` change.

**Trigger:** a note with `maturity: capture` that I use now · **Input:** one note · **Driven by:** `/guide-distillation`

## Steps

- Contextualise: links, backlinks, `paras`, type and subtypes.
- Diagnose by the kind of the note:
	- Summary form: summary, abstract, definition or take-aways. An active decision ([[How I Like My Notes]]).
	- Format of the kind: LaTeX for concepts, bullets for indexes, prose for MoCs.
	- Noise to prune, typos, broken or missing links.
	- Indexes or MoCs that must link it.
- Approve the new top callouts and the list of changes.
- Apply. Keep my wording where it is clear. Bold only in long notes, on the phrases I would scan for.
- A note that holds two ideas: propose the split, never split alone. Concepts go through `/new-concept`.
- `maturity: capture` → `distilled`. The note keeps its folder.
- Offer the index or MoC link when it is missing.

## Tidies

- Raw captures, orphans, broken links of the note.
