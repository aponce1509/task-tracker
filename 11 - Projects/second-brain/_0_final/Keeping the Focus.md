# [[Keeping the Focus]]

> [!abstract] Stay on the task, park the rest
> - Flow: a fixed place for distractions, a plan for the day and the week, one task at a time, a bridge at the end.
> - Techniques: the archipelago to start, the scope dial to finish, the Hemingway bridge to resume.
> - The workplace and the day workflows implement it.

## The problem

I work on one concrete task and an idea comes to my mind, or I see something that could be better. I start to think about it and the task stops. Focus is the convergence mode of [[Convergence and Divergence]]; it must stay on while I execute.

## The rules

1. Any random idea has one entry point: the daily note. At the end of the day the entries move to their place: ideas, issues ([[The Issue System]]), prompts. [[WORKFLOWS - End Day]] sweeps them.
2. The week has a preview and a focus: [[WORKFLOWS - Start Week]].
3. The tasks of the day are mandatory: [[WORKFLOWS - Start Day]] writes the plan in the daily note.
4. Focus on the task. That's it.
5. For work at home, block the time and list the tasks before starting: [[Being productive at home - Task Blocks]].

## The techniques

- To start without a blank page, fill the document with the material first: [[The Archipelago of Ideas]].
- To finish, reduce the breadth and ship something small: [[Dial Down the Scope]].
- To resume without friction, stop when the next step is clear and write it down: [[The Hemingway Bridge]]. In the vault it is the `bridge` property of the PAR notes.

## The implementation

The workplace keeps the plan, the bridges and the active projects in front of me: [[The Workplace System]] (the `mise-en-place` plugin, the Today dashboard). The old draft of the rules is [[Alejandro's Mise-en-Place]]. The tasks themselves live in [[Task Management]].
