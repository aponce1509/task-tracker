# [[Express - CODE]]

> [!important] 
> See: [[Intermediate and Final Products]]
## Definition

Expressing is the act of sharing your work and ideas with the world (or your team). In the context of a developer in a large organization, it is the shift from private consumption to the creation of [[Intermediate and Final Products|intermediate products]] or final deliverables. It represents the ultimate phase of [[Convergence and Divergence|Convergence]].

## The Express Mindset for Developers

1. **Think in Small Pieces**: Don't wait until a project is finished to "Express." Release small, reusable chunks (Intermediate Packets) to reduce risk and get feedback: [[Dial Down the Scope]]
2. **Build for Future You**: Treat your code, documentation, and logic as products that your "future self" or a colleague will "consume."
3. **Move from Research to Production**: Expressing is the moment where gathered data transforms into a decision, a feature, or a fix.
