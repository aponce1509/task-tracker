# [[Postmortem Content]]

> [!abstract] The question bank of every post-mortem
> - Performance: the objective it moved, the good, the bad, a rate from 1 to 3.
> - Notes to create: the ending note first, then the reusable parts, tech debt and the rest.
> - Used when I close an issue ([[WORKFLOWS - Complete Issue]]) and when I close a PAR ([[WORKFLOWS - End PAR]]).

## Performance

- Which personal or work objective it moves.
- List of good things.
- List of bad things.
- Rate from 1 to 3.

## Notes to create

- Ending note: the core note or index that builds the final solution.
- Things I can re-use in another place.
- Tech debt note.
- Review of the resources and tools used.
- Additional requirements.
- Ideas.

## The ending note

The ending note is the index or MoC of the final solution of a PAR. It shapes the final document and is valid for any kind of work. I read it when I look for reusable content.

- Build it from the bases queries of the PAR note: they give the material for an ordered index of the "book" of the project, area or resource.
- First the notes that build the final solution.
- Then the knowledge I need to understand the whole product.
- Mark the intermediate products: [[Intermediate and Final Products]].
- The problems, the solutions and the decisions are the narrative.

It is an index or a MoC, so the `note-index-moc` skill writes it ([[The Index and MoC System]]).

Part of [[The PARA System]] and [[Task Management]].
