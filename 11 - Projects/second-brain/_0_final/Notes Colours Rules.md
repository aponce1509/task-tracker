# [[Notes Colours Rules]]

> [!abstract] One colour per role of the highlight
> - Red and blue first: what is critical and the questions.
> - Then yellow: the evidence.
> - Then green: the cards to learn.

## The colours

- <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Red</mark>: critical, the most important take-away of the source. It can be anything; the explanation of why goes right after the highlight.
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Blue</mark>: questions.
- <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Yellow</mark>: simple remarks (evidence).
- Purple: conclusions.
- Green: cards to learn.
- Orange: author indexing.
- Gray: examples and exercises.
- Pink: references.

## The order

A text should read as a sequence: red or blue → yellow → green.
