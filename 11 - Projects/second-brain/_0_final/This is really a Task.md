# [[This is really a Task]]

> [!abstract] Think in issues and their tasks
> - A task is a piece of work that gives one block of work.
> - It is an issue with a fancy name and some requirements.
> - The key of my productivity: I prepare my mind with issues and the tasks of each issue.

## Definition

A "real task" is a task that gives a block of work: one sitting, one small packet, a clear end. In the vault it is an issue ([[The Issue System]]) and its `- [ ]` tasks.

> [!important]
> - The key to productivity for me is to think in issues and the tasks of each issue, not in projects.

Small packets keep me on one thing at a time — [[Keeping the Focus]] · [[TODO list for your daily work]]. The issues come first: [[RULE - Create notes for the issues]].
