# [[Progressive Summarisation]]

## Definition

A technique for "layering" a note over time. You only add a layer of summarisation when you revisit the note, ensuring that the most frequently used notes become the most refined.

## The Layers of Summarisation

1. **Layer 0**: The raw capture (the full text or original highlights).
2. **Layer 1 (Bold)**: Bold the most important sentences or phrases within the text.
3. **Layer 2 (Highlight)**: Use a digital highlighter on the best parts of the **bolded** text.
4. **Layer 3 (Executive Summary)**: Write a bulleted summary at the top of the note in your own words.

## Benefits

1. **Context Preservation**: You never lose the original source, as the raw text stays underneath the highlights.
2. **Speed**: It allows for "glanceable" notes that can be understood in under 10 seconds.
3. **Efficiency**: You only invest effort in notes that you actually re-read.

## When the layers happen

- The first layer may land while organising ([[Organise - CODE]]): more context, summary lines, first bullets.
- The deeper layers come with each distillation revisit ([[Distill - CODE]]), never at capture time.
