# [[Intermediate and Final Products]]

## Definition

These are the "Lego bricks" of your work. By breaking large projects into these smaller units, you ensure that even if a project is cancelled, the value created remains accessible.

## The Catalog of Outputs

1. Conceptual & Strategic (Intermediate)
	1. **Distilled Notes**: Curated insights from external sources or internal brainstorming.
	2. **Outtakes**: Strategic leftovers—analysis or data that didn't fit the current scope but remains valuable.
	3. **Decision Logs (ADRs)**: Documentation explaining *why* a specific technical decision was made.
	4. **Questions & Doubts**: Structured inquiries used to probe system architecture.
2. Technical & Code Assets (Intermediate/Final)
	1. **Scripts & Algorithms**: Small automation tools or optimized logic blocks.
	2. **Internal Libraries**: Reusable code modules extracted from a specific feature.
	3. **Design Patterns**: Documented templates for solving recurring architectural problems.
	4. **Prompts & Agents**: System instructions and AI configurations developed for specific tasks.
3. Project Logistics & Definition (Intermediate)
	1. **Requirements (PRDs)**: Product definitions, feature sets, or job descriptions.
	2. **Technical Debt Log**: A clear map of what needs fixing to avoid future "interest" payments.
	3. **Workflows & Models**: Diagrams or descriptions of how data and users move through a system.
	4. **Agenda & Meeting Outcomes**: Results from synchronizations that drive the next action.
4. Final Deliverables (Final Products)
	1. **Final Documents**: PDFs, Word docs, or Slides for stakeholders.
	2. **Core Components**: The primary, functional feature or system being shipped.
	3. **Implementation Details**: The "How-To" guide for deployment and maintenance.
	4. **Curriculums/Reviews**: Comprehensive overviews of a subject or project performance.

## Suggested Additions for Big Tech

1. **Post-Mortems / RCAs**: Analysis of failures to prevent recurrence (High-value IP).
2. **Proof of Concepts (PoC)**: A minimal implementation to prove a technical hypothesis.
3. **RFCs (Request for Comments)**: A collaborative document to gather feedback before implementation.
4. **Onboarding Guides**: A "Packet" designed to get a new dev up to speed on your specific codebase.
