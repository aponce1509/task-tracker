# [[Managing online References]]

> [!abstract] Three kinds of reference, three places
> - Important for learning: needed to do a task or to learn; always stored in Zotero.
> - Leisure and casual reading: for the moments I do not want to learn.
> - Reader is only an inbox and the app to consume the internet.

## The three kinds

- **Important for learning**: the references needed to do a task or to learn something. They always go to Zotero ([[Zotero - The ultimate reference manager]]).
- **Leisure**: content to enjoy. Books, films and shows go to the media database ([[References Database]]).
- **Casual reading**: articles and posts with no goal.

There are moments when I do not want to learn. Then I consume leisure or casual reading: commuting, bed time or just resting.

## Reader

Reader is used only as an inbox and as the app to consume the internet. A reference that turns out to be important for learning moves to Zotero.
