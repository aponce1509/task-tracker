> [!Summary]
> This note implements how we do organise in our project:
> - [[Organise#Rules]]
> - [[Organise#Implementation Notes]]

Consist in the workflows of managing all the captures for future consumption. This task have two main benefits:

- Place the things so you can use it later
- When organising, you make a second thought about the capture you took
	- Maybe it is just trash
- Complete the metadata

The features requested for searchability:

- tagged and with metadata
- Easy to collect from your PARA Database

This should be done:

- Every Week for content and ideas that arrive to your mind/life
- After ending your work block
- After a book-unit is complete
- After a writing session

## Rules

- Complete the [[CheckList - Out Of Inbox]]
- Don't waste time here cleaning/formatting the note
- Answer to the question of the next subsection
### Questions

- Is this note useful? If not, remove
- Where it belongs? Assign [[PARA Note]]
- Is the note name interesting? Note names should generate emotions
