# [[Gym Progression]]

### When to Increase Weight

You should increase weight when:

- You can perform the final reps of your last set with little effort or no form breakdown.
- You exceed your target rep range consistently (e.g., aiming for 8–12 but doing 13–14 easily).
- You no longer feel challenged, and there’s no muscle fatigue or pump after sets.

**Guideline:** Increase weight by 2.5–10% depending on the exercise. Compound lifts (e.g., squats, deadlifts) tolerate larger jumps than isolation movements (e.g., curls).
