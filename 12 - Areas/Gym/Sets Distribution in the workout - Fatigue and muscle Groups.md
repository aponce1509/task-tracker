# [[Sets Distribution in the workout - Fatigue and muscle Groups]]


> [!important] Take-aways
> - 2–3 sessions/week per muscle group
> - Try to have high/low intensity days
> - 10-20 Sets per muscle groups

- **For muscle growth (hypertrophy):**  
    **10–20 sets** per muscle group per week, ideally spread over 2–3 sessions.
- **For strength:**  
    **8–12 sets** per muscle group per week with heavier loads (e.g., 4–6 reps per set).

Es importante añadir variaciones que se centren en distintas partes y tipos de movimiento

## Fatigue

Depends on the type of the training goal

| Training Goal | Typical Recovery Time per Muscle Group |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Strength      | 48–72 hours                            |
| Hypertrophy   | 24–48 hours                            |
| Endurance     | 24 hours or less                       |

**Note:** These are general estimates. Recovery varies by:

- **Training volume and intensity**
- **Muscle group size** (e.g., lower body often requires more recovery)
- **Individual recovery ability** (sleep, nutrition, age, stress)
