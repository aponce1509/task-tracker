The risk-neutral valuation principle states that a derivative can be valued by

from the underlying asset equals the risk-free interest rate and (b) discounting
the expected payoff at the risk-free interest rate.” Yellow Highlight

Martingales and measures are critical to a full understanding of risk neutral
valuation. A martingale is a zero-drift stochastic process. A measure is the
unit in which we value security prices. A key result in this chapter will be the
equivalent martingale measure result. This states that if we use the price of a
traded security as the unit of measurement then there is a market price of risk
for which all security prices follow martingales.” Yellow Highlight
