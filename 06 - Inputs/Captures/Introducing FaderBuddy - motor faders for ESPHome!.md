> - [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l-DIM_kQ8EY&t=0s) This is FaderBuddy, the easiest way to add motor faders to your ESPHome projects With remote fader control and software-defined virtual detents, it's now simple to create intuitive physical interfaces for your smart home or DIY devices. Set the number of detents to 1 and you even get a spring-loaded return-to-center behavior. All of this takes just a few lines of yaml code to integrate, and the whole project is open-source so you can build your own.
