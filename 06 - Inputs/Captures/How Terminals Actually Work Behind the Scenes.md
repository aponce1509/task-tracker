# [[How Terminals Actually Work Behind the Scenes]]

> [!abstract] Video by [[@MohidoTech]]
> - Terminals evolved from physical teletypes to modern virtual consoles managed by the Linux kernel's TTY driver.
> - The TTY module buffers input and echoes characters before forwarding data to applications.
> - Pseudo-terminals enable hundreds of simultaneous sessions while emulators fork child processes like Bash.
