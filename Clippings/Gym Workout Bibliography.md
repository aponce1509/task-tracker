Certainly. Here's a concise list of reputable books on gym workouts, covering foundational knowledge, technique, and program design:

### Beginner to Intermediate Level

1. ***Starting Strength* by Mark Rippetoe**
	- Focus: Strength training fundamentals and barbell technique
	- Highly recommended for beginners
2. ***The New Rules of Lifting* by Lou Schuler & Alwyn Cosgrove**
	- Focus: General fitness, fat loss, and strength programming
3. ***Strength Training Anatomy* by Frédéric Delavier**
	- Focus: Muscle anatomy with detailed illustrations and exercise guides

### Intermediate to Advanced

1. ***Practical Programming for Strength Training* by Mark Rippetoe & Andy Baker**
	- Focus: Program periodization and strength development
2. ***Science and Development of Muscle Hypertrophy* by Brad Schoenfeld**
	- Focus: Evidence-based approach to muscle growth
3. ***Bigger Leaner Stronger* by Michael Matthews**
	- Focus: Science-backed methods for building muscle and burning fat
